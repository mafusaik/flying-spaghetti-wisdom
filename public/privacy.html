Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Flying Spaghetti Wisdom, developed by GlazerDev ("we", "us", or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information that may be processed in connection with your use of the Flying Spaghetti Wisdom mobile application and related web pages.

May His Noodly Appendage touch your heart; our commitment is simple: we prioritize minimal data collection, transparency, and user privacy.

2. Information We Collect

Local App Preferences: Flying Spaghetti Wisdom stores user preferences (such as notification time preferences, selected app language, and daily reading progress) directly on your device using local storage or device settings. This information is not transmitted to our servers.

Personal Data: We do not require you to create an account, log in with an email, or submit sensitive personally identifiable information to use the core features of the app (reading the Gospel, viewing daily wisdom, or receiving reminders).

Support Communications: If you contact us via email at glazer.dev@gmail.com, we will receive your email address and any content included in your message solely to respond to your support request.

3. Notifications and Local Reminders

When you enable "Daily Reminders" in the app, local notifications are scheduled natively on your device operating system (iOS or Android). These reminders are triggered locally without tracking your location or sending telemetry back to remote servers.

4. Third-Party Services

The application is distributed through official app stores (such as Google Play Store and Apple App Store). These platforms may collect basic crash reports or anonymized aggregate installation metrics in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this document. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
